FIND MY HOME · EXPERIMENTAL

Your things, together.

Choose what to track. See when it was last found.

Connecting…

Locations are last sightings, not live GPS. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. Your laptop can be off.

01 · CONNECT

Connect your Apple account

Used to refresh locations. Your password is discarded after sign-in. If Apple expires the session, Home Assistant will ask you to reconnect.

02 · DISCOVER

Find your items

Recovery uses a separate Apple session. You may need to sign in again, choose a trusted device, and enter that device’s unlock passcode.

03 · CHOOSE

Track only what you choose

Newly discovered items start off. Existing choices survive rediscovery. To add an item later, discover again and select it here.

Home Assistant connection

After installing the companion integration, approve the discovered Find My Home entry in Settings → Devices & services. It creates separate Beta entities and uses Home Assistant’s normal zones and Recorder history.

Battery levels and street addresses are not supplied by this source. This beta does not reverse-geocode your locations or invent a battery percentage.

Private account state stays in this app. No credentials in options, logs, diagnostics or browser storage. Protect your Home Assistant account and backups.
